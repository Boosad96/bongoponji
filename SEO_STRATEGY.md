# SEO strategy

Canonicalize by search intent. The generic yearly pages serve Gregorian month browsing with Bangladesh civil equivalents. Bengali-year pages organize the same rules by Bengali month and date. Preserve address paths when adding a new supported year. The regional calendar alias follows the primary 2027 calendar page.

Set SITE_URL to https://bongoponji.com on the approved production deployment. Leave it unset on the initial Pages review site and preview builds. Without it, all pages are noindexed and the robots file blocks crawling. The sitemap lists the homepage and completed civil or methodology pages. Search-based daily selections and pending observances are noindexed. Hreflang becomes eligible when true locale versions exist. Submit the sitemap through Search Console once production ownership and public URLs are verified.

See KEYWORD_MAP.csv and COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md for intent mapping and evidence limits. Start with month-specific calendars and conversion queries. Add regional comparisons and observances only after they provide independently verified information. The year and route factory supports expansion within the civil calendar range. Google Search Console metrics and Core Web Vitals measurements are pending deployment.
