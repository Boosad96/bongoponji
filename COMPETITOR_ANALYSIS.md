# Competitor analysis

Public search and page review, 2026-11-27. The observations below are qualitative. Search volume, CPC, difficulty, ranks and traffic were unavailable.

- Drik Panchang exposes year- and city-specific lists for Bengali festivals, with event types and relevant tithis. This shows established intent for a festival-year-city result. A Bengali-first page with explicit source, tradition and verification dates would answer a different set of reader questions. [Kolkata festival calendar](https://www.drikpanchang.com/bengali/calendar/bengali-calendar.html?geoname-id=1275004&year=2027)
- BengaliCalendar.com presented an access verification interstitial. Its page navigation and features remain unassessed. [Public entry point](https://bengalicalendar.com/)
- GetBangla emphasizes Bangladesh's civil-calendar rules and a date converter. Reacting to the Bangladesh and West Bengal distinction directly on converter and festival pages is a candidate content opportunity. [GetBangla calendar](https://www.getbangla.com/en/bangla-calendar/)
- BanglaDateToday provides a page explaining fixed civic dates and leap handling. Transparent examples and a Bengali-only reading path remain useful competitive targets. [Methodology](https://www.bangladatetoday.com/methodology)

Searches surfaced annual calendar hubs, daily pages, converters and future festival explainers. Candidate gaps include a two-way converter that accepts Bengali numerals, a comparison of regional methods for the same civil day, and a source-backed birthday result. Validate demand after Search Console has enough impressions. From annual hubs, link to month grids, relevant observance pages after approval, and the date converter. From festival explainers, link to the method and any independently verified regional variation.
