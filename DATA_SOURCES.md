# Data sources

- India Meteorological Department, Positional Astronomy Centre, Kolkata. The regional table gives Bengal and Assam solar month starts. The horizon table provides minute-resolution comparison rows. The disclaimer establishes public access and leaves reuse rights unresolved. Keep source table extracts internal until clearance. [Regional calendar](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/panchang/en/regional-calendars) · [Horizon table](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/panchang/en/tables) · [Disclaimer](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/disclaimer.php)
- Ramakrishna Mission's Panjika Reader describes the Bishuddha Siddhanta printed calendar, with annotated examples of sunrise, tithi and traditional time units. Its Ashwin dates corroborate the PAC table. The related Kolkata printed samples differ from PAC at Srabon and Poush. The Kundali Study describes the Kolkata mean-midnight rule and a calibration, which the research CLI uses without importing the study's code. Complete edition coverage and a computable observance specification remain outstanding. [Panjika Reader](https://cs.rkmvu.ac.in/~tamal/jyotisha/panjika/) · [Printed sample notes](https://cs.rkmvu.ac.in/~tamal/jyotisha/barbela.html) · [Kundali Study](https://cs.rkmvu.ac.in/~tamal/jyotisha/kundali/)
- Bangladesh civil calendar. The government-hosted Teachers' Portal describes the revised month lengths. Contemporary reporting quotes the revision committee and documents the civic-date anchors. The Teachers' Portal article is a contributor explanation. The official holiday notice is a further cross-check candidate. The application independently computes dates from the rules. [Teachers' Portal](https://teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/593283) · [Contemporary report](https://bdnews24.com/lifestyle/bangladesh-reworks-bangla-calendar-to-match-national-days-with-west) · [2027 holiday notice](https://mopa.gov.bd/pages/public-holiday)
- Astronomy Engine supplies the computational base and carries an MIT licence. [Source and licence](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy)
- Drik Panchang was consulted for public product structure only. Grahan is a candidate for future methodology review. Neither supplies records to the current publication dataset. [Drik Panchang](https://www.drikpanchang.com/bengali/calendar/bengali-calendar.html?geoname-id=1275004&year=2027) · [Grahan](https://github.com/svarbhanu/grahan)
