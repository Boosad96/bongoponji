# Known limitations

- Daily Panchang coverage consists of three checked Kolkata samples for 27–29 November 2026. Other dates and cities show unavailable status. Coordinates and independent comparisons outside Kolkata are pending.
- West Bengal conversion remains internal and range-limited. PAC clearance and additional year boundaries are pending. Bishuddha Siddhanta and Gupta Press publication claims require complete rules and comparisons.
- Bangladesh mode applies rule-level validation to the revised civil calendar. It covers October 2019 to December 2099. Future individual government calendar editions have yet to be cross-checked.
- Tithi has an internal transition test. Nakshatra, yoga, karana, sankranti, religious observance logic, regional disagreements, approved festival dates and marriage dates are pending.
- Remaining P1 work includes widgets, automated PDF and ICS, religious event calendars, birthday and age utilities, countdowns and installable PWA. Calendar pages include browser print styles. Telegram, admin, monetization and automatic approved-dataset promotion are pending.
- The correction log is public. Sending reports requires configuring an owner-managed email. No private report store is active.
- Managed preview and Playwright could not start a web server. No screenshots, browser accessibility assessment, Lighthouse runs, Core Web Vitals data or live rate-limit configuration were verified.
- The source repository is public on GitHub. Pages deployment, production domain and Search Console ownership are pending.
