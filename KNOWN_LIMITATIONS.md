December 4, 9, 15 and 27 remain withheld, as do January 4, 7, 17, 20, 24, 27, 30 and 31. Other dates and cities show unavailable status. Coordinates and independent comparisons outside Kolkata are pending.
- West Bengal conversion remains internal and range-limited. PAC reuse clearance and full printed 1433–1434 boundary coverage are pending. Two 1433 PAC dates disagree with the RKM printed examples, and the Barrackpore institutional schedule corroborates 1 Baishakh 1434 only. Eleven 1434 boundaries await publication or access to an authoritative reference. The Grahan comparison supplies additional astronomical evidence only. PAC-based batches reach 20 April 2027. The remainder of April and all of May lack an applicable PAC horizon and tithi reference. The consecutive validation stops at May until the 1949 Saka edition or equivalent PAC evidence is accessible. Bishuddha Siddhanta and Gupta Press publication claims require complete rules and comparisons.
- Bangladesh mode applies rule-level validation to the revised civil calendar. It covers October 2019 to December 2099. Future individual government calendar editions have yet to be cross-checked.
- Tithi has an internal transition test. Nakshatra, yoga, karana, sankranti, religious observance logic, regional disagreements, approved festival dates and marriage dates are pending.
