# Known limitations

- Daily Panchang coverage consists of 45 checked Kolkata records: 27 November–3 December 2026, 22 additional December dates and 16 January 2027 dates. 4, 9, 12, 15, 27 and 29 December remain withheld. Other dates and cities show unavailable status. Coordinates and independent comparisons outside Kolkata are pending.
- West Bengal conversion remains internal and range-limited. PAC reuse clearance and full printed 1433–1434 boundary coverage are pending. Two 1433 PAC dates disagree with the RKM printed examples, and the Barrackpore institutional schedule corroborates 1 Baishakh 1434 only. Eleven 1434 boundaries await publication or access to an authoritative reference. The Grahan comparison supplies additional astronomical evidence only. The December 2026 and January 2027 PAC-based batches were explicitly authorized. Later daily batches remain gated. Bishuddha Siddhanta and Gupta Press publication claims require complete rules and comparisons.
- Bangladesh mode applies rule-level validation to the revised civil calendar. It covers October 2019 to December 2099. Future individual government calendar editions have yet to be cross-checked.
- Tithi has an internal transition test. Nakshatra, yoga, karana, sankranti, religious observance logic, regional disagreements, approved festival dates and marriage dates are pending.
- Remaining P1 work includes widgets, automated PDF and ICS, religious event calendars, birthday and age utilities, countdowns and installable PWA. Calendar pages include browser print styles. Telegram, admin, monetization and automatic approved-dataset promotion are pending.
- The correction log is public. Sending reports requires configuring an owner-managed email. No private report store is active.
- The managed preview could not start a web server. GitHub Actions provides Chromium browser coverage. No screenshots, browser accessibility assessment, Lighthouse runs, Core Web Vitals data or live rate-limit configuration were verified.
- The source repository is public on GitHub. Pages deployment, production domain and Search Console ownership are pending.
